Rail siding: the first wagon dispatches to the port

We worked out the volume at which a wagon beats the truck fleet, and where the saving starts for the buyer.

The first wagon consignments

The rail siding lets us assemble large consignments without an extra transfer through an intermediate store. The first departures ran to the port terminals in Odesa.

Every wagon is weighed, sealed and issued its accompanying documents on the day of departure.

When rail is more efficient

For small urgent volumes, the truck fleet remains more flexible. Rail becomes efficient when a buyer consolidates a stable volume and can receive it in one delivery window.

  • Fewer intermediate grain-handling operations.
  • More stable unit cost over long distances.
  • One laboratory and document trail.

Route control

The manager tracks the consignment from silo to destination station. If an infrastructure slot changes, the partner is updated before departure rather than after a delay.
