Soybean

What is actually on offer

Non-GMO soybean of merchantable grade with confirmed origin: every lot is traceable to a specific field and harvest date. That is not a label claim — it rests on seed purchased with documented varietal purity, and on soybean fields physically separated from any GM crop in our own rotation and from neighbouring farms by a buffer strip.

The 2026 season closed at 2.9 t/ha across 1,500 hectares — 4,350 tonnes gross. Soybean is a small share of our 14,200 hectares, and that scale is exactly why we can keep it under full segregation: one stream from field to storage cell, with no commingling of other grain at any stage.

  • Seed with documented varietal purity and no GM component.
  • Buffer strip and dedicated equipment at sowing and harvest.
  • Every lot traces back to its field of origin and harvest date.

Identity preservation: how we prove it

Identity preservation means our soybean never touches a combine, trailer or storage cell that has handled a GM crop that day or the day before, until the line has gone through a documented purge and clean-down. Every clean-down is logged with a timestamp — part of the lot file, not a verbal understanding between crews.

Seed is bought from a supplier who provides a document on varietal identity and the absence of a GM component; that document sits in the lot file alongside the laboratory certificates. Sampling at every stage — from the truck on the weighbridge to the storage cell — is logged with a date, lot number and the responsible agronomist's name.

A buyer paying a premium for non-GMO status is paying for exactly this chain of records, not for one certificate at the end. We show the sampling log and the lot's route from field to truck as a standard part of the document package, not as an exception granted on request.

  • Equipment purge and clean-down between GM and non-GM streams, time-stamped.
  • Supplier document on varietal identity kept in the lot file.
  • Sampling log from field to dispatch available to the buyer.

Laboratory and lot certificate

Analysis is done in our own laboratory at the elevator. Every truck coming off the field passes intake control for moisture, foreign matter and the share of split or damaged beans. A completed lot then receives a full certificate: protein on a dry matter basis, oil content, urease activity, test weight and moisture.

We monitor urease activity because it indicates thermal or mechanical damage to the bean during handling: a reading outside 0.1–0.4 pH rise (the standard trypsin-inhibitor proxy under ISO 14902, referenced alongside DSTU 4230) points to overheating during drying, which is critical rather than merely informational for soybean destined for food-grade or seed use.

The buyer receives the certificate before dispatch. Alongside it comes the non-GMO confirmation and an extract from the sampling log for that specific lot. If a buyer needs an independent PCR test from an accredited laboratory, we provide a sample for it without holding up the shipment.

Storage and drying

Our own elevator holds 46,000 tonnes; with leased capacity the total is 58,000 tonnes. Drying runs at 1,200 tonnes per day across three units. Soybean is dried carefully and in smaller batches at a time: the bean splits easily and loses merchantable appearance under an aggressive temperature regime, so our drying rate for soybean runs lower than for cereals.

Soybean is held in a dedicated group of storage cells that carry no GM crop in the current season. That costs more to administer than shared storage, but it is what keeps the lot clean from one loading to the next — not just clean on paper.

  • 46,000 t own storage, 58,000 t including leased capacity.
  • A dedicated cell group reserved for the non-GMO stream.
  • Reduced drying temperature to preserve bean integrity.

Logistics and dispatch

The elevator stands in Soroka, Vinnytsia region: 35 km to Haisyn, 85 km to Vinnytsia, 230 km to Kyiv and 380 km to the port of Odesa. The port leg is a single run, with no transhipment through an intermediate warehouse.

Dispatch is by road, on your trucks or ours. Before loading, every truck taking soybean goes through the same clean-down check applied at intake: the non-GMO stream does not cross paths with other grain even in transport. A lot from 100 tonnes is scheduled for loading within three working days of agreement.

Commercial terms

We work under contract, VAT registered, with a complete document package that includes non-GMO confirmation and a laboratory certificate for every lot. Minimum lot is 100 tonnes. Spot payment is 3–5 banking days after acceptance; on a forward, an advance and a payment schedule are fixed in the contract before sowing.

For us soybean is not only a cash crop but the preceding crop ahead of winter wheat: as a legume it fixes nitrogen in the soil, which is why the following wheat field establishes more evenly. Since 2008 we have shipped grain to buyers in 14 countries, and on every soybean contract the origin documents are prepared by our own accounts and agronomy teams, not by an intermediary.
