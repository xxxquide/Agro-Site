Privacy Policy

This page explains what data the CENTRAGRO PLUS LLC website processes, why, and what happens to it once the site's forms are connected to a real handler.

Last updated 6 August 2026

1. What the site's forms collect

The site has exactly two forms, and they are the only places a visitor enters personal data.

The contact form asks for name, email, crop (a dropdown), volume (free text), and message (free text). The newsletter form in the footer asks only for an email address.

No other part of the site collects personal data: the company, crops, and contact sections only display content that was not entered by you.

2. This is a demo site: nothing is transmitted

Neither form has a backend. Whatever you type is not transmitted, stored, or processed by us or by anyone else. After a valid submission, an on-screen notice says exactly that.

This is a deliberate feature of a demonstration layout, not an oversight or a hidden mechanism. We state it plainly so nobody assumes their data is being kept somewhere when it is not.

3. Purpose and legal basis once a handler is connected

When the forms are connected to a real handler, the sole purpose of processing will be to handle a commercial enquiry: to contact you back, clarify details about the crop and volume, and respond. Data will not be used for profiling, unsolicited marketing, or transfers to third parties.

The legal basis will be the data subject's consent, given by completing and submitting the form, under Ukraine's Law on Personal Data Protection (Закон України «Про захист персональних даних»). For enquiries originating from the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) will also apply as the framework governing that consent and the associated rights.

4. Retention period and data controller

Once a handler is connected, contact-form data will be kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting correspondence, and in any case no longer than 12 months from the last contact, after which it will be deleted. Email addresses collected through the newsletter form will be kept until the subscriber unsubscribes or withdraws consent.

The data controller will be CENTRAGRO PLUS LLC, 4 Zarichna St, Soroka, Vinnytsia region, 22731, Ukraine. Data will not be shared with third parties except where required by law.

5. Cookies, analytics, and third-party requests

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising services, and makes no requests to third-party servers. Fonts, scripts, and images are all served from the site's own hosting.

In practice, your browser loads nothing beyond the site itself while you browse it, and no browsing behaviour is tracked in any form.

6. Your rights as a data subject

Under Ukraine's Law on Personal Data Protection, you have the right to know that your data is being processed, to access it, to request correction or deletion, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with Ukraine's Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@centragro.example describing your request. We respond within our working hours, Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00, and provide a substantive reply within 30 days.

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (the "right to be forgotten")
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Human Rights

7. Changes to this policy

If our approach to data processing changes — in particular once a real handler is connected to the forms — we will update this page and the date shown at the top. Material changes affecting your rights will be flagged on this page for 30 days after publication.

Continuing to use the site after an updated version is published means you have had the opportunity to review the current policy.
